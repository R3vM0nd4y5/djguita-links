About Yukiyu Records

Named for Yukiyu, the good spirit of El Yunque, the rainforest of Puerto Rico. The spelling is ours. Founded 2026 in Aurora, Illinois. One artist, every song hers, every number read in-house.

Founder and CEO

Olga Homar Olmeda

The songwriter behind every Yukiyu Records release: Brujeria, Angel Face, Your shoes next to mine, Time Flies, and Demons In My Closet. An ASCAP writer and publisher, she turns feelings into frequencies and writes songs through love, memory and time. DJGuita is her artist name, and Yukiyu Records is her house.

CMO and CTO, the social-business manager

Jeffrey Reeves

The engineer behind the label. A derivatives trader by trade, raised on the floors of the Chicago Board Options Exchange and the Board of Trade, Carnegie Mellon's Tepper School before that. He brought the trading desk's habits to a record label: measure everything, mark it to market every day, and never pay twice for what you can build once. He builds the machinery: one card that puts a release on every page at once, the numbers desk that reads every stream and every dollar, and this site. By night an ASCAP writer himself.
