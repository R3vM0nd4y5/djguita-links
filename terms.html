DJGuita publishing — Terms of Service

Effective November 24, 2026. Yukiyu Records LLC, the publishing house of the artist DJGuita.

Who may use it

"DJGuita publishing" is a private application operated by Yukiyu Records LLC to publish the artist DJGuita's own videos to her own YouTube channel. It is used only by the artist and the people working for her label, on accounts they own or administer. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

On the channel owner's consent, it uploads videos, sets their details and privacy, and manages playlists on that channel through the YouTube Data API. Every upload is one the artist has approved. The application never posts, edits or deletes anything the artist has not asked for.

Your accounts, your content

The artist keeps all rights to her music, videos, artwork and channel. The application holds no license to them beyond doing the publishing it is asked to do. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
